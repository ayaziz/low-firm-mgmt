# Phase 3 Vision and Metrics

## Vision Statement
Phase 3 turns LOMA into a **governed legal-operations platform** where enriched data, transparent workflow history, and proactive alerts support faster and safer decisions.

## Strategic Objectives

| Objective | Description | KPI | Target |
|---|---|---|---|
| Data Completeness | Enrich legal and customer records for operational decisions. | % active cases with risk+priority+opposing counsel filled | >=95% |
| Governance | Enforce approvals in finance and sensitive scheduling actions. | % invoices/wages bypassing approval | 0% |
| Transparency | Expose status timelines across all status-bearing entities. | % in-scope entities with visible timeline | 100% |
| DMS Maturity | Improve findability and lifecycle management. | Median document retrieval time | -40% vs P2 baseline |
| Notification Reliability | Deliver reminders and approval alerts on time. | Alert dispatch success before SLA window | >=99% |
| Analytics Adoption | Reduce dependence on manual exports. | Weekly active admin/lawyer users of dashboard KPIs | >=70% of target roles |

## North-Star User Outcomes
- Lawyers resolve scheduling and case triage faster due to richer context.
- Accountants process invoicing/wages with clear separation of duties.
- Tenant admins monitor receivables, workload, and compliance from one dashboard.

## Measurement Cadence
- Weekly product telemetry review (engineering + product).
- Monthly tenant health review with trend lines and corrective actions.
